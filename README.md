# BlasterCaddy
The Blaster Caddy is a 3D printed tool to temporarily mount a Buckthorn Blaster to loppers.

To 3D print:
1. STL units are in mm
2. Longest dimension is 180 mm
3. Infill should be 30-40% to provide enough strength and flexibility

To use:
1. Put a piece of double-sided tape on the back surface of the Blaster Caddy, position as shown in the image, and stick it to the lopper handle.
2. Holding the Blaster Caddy in place, attach two zip ties in the grooves and tighten the zip ties as tightly as possible
   
Note: When lopping, avoid putting excess force directly on the flanges that hold the Buckthorn Blaster as they can break
